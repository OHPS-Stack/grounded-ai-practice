# Grounded AI Practice — Logo Symbol Creative Brief

Working brief for external image-generation workflows (not a project research
document — see `project_brief.md` for the project's actual working decisions;
this file just packages them for portability).

## Project in one line

Grounded AI Practice — a research-led project teaching practical, responsible
AI capability to everyday individuals and SME employees, positioned against
hype-driven AI marketing: credible and evidence-based, not a tech-startup
product.

## What's needed

Polished **symbol/icon concepts only** — not a full logo lockup, not a
wordmark. The wordmark is being solved separately. Deliver each concept as a
clean, single-colour-capable vector-style mark (even if generated as a raster
image) — no embedded text.

## Tone

Between "grounded/academic" and "approachable/friendly." Credible,
evidence-led, restrained — but not dry, institutional, or intimidating to a
general-public audience with no technical background.

## Palette (use exactly these; do not substitute)

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#27221E` | primary mark colour (dark, warm near-black — not pure black) |
| Ember | `#F15E4B` | single accent colour — use sparingly, one element per mark |
| Paper | `#F9F9F9` | background |

(Sand `#F9E8DC`, Mist `#EFEEED`, Sage `#D5E2E1` exist in the wider brand
palette for backgrounds/sections but are not expected in the icon itself.)

## Style reference

A prior mark in this project's history (not to be copied, but matches the
craft level and style being aimed for) fused two ideas into one clean
silhouette: an open book/journal shape containing a terminal prompt
(`>` chevron + cursor), flat colour, bold rounded strokes, no gradients, no
drop shadows or 3D effects, reads clearly even at favicon size (down to
~32px). That combination of **conceptual fusion + flat geometric execution**
is the bar — two ideas merged into one legible shape, not a literal
illustration of either.

## Candidate concept directions

Any of these, blended, or genuinely new directions in the same spirit are
welcome — these are starting points, not a spec:

1. **Hand + spark** — an open hand/palm supporting a small spark or node.
   Human oversight holding AI, not the reverse.
2. **Lens + spark** — a magnifying glass with a small spark/mark inside the
   lens. Verifying or examining AI output rather than accepting it blindly.
3. **Tree from a chip/base** — a small circuit-board or chip base with a
   tree or growth shape rising from it. Growth and practice rooted in a
   technical foundation.
4. **Stepping stones** — a short ascending sequence of simple shapes
   (squares/stones), the last one filled solid. Practice as a sequential,
   modular path rather than one big leap.
5. **Compass** — a compass body with a two-tone needle (one tip in Ember).
   Practical direction/guidance through AI complexity, not a black box.
6. **Bookmark + check** — a bookmark/ribbon shape with a checkmark cut
   through it. Returning practice plus verified understanding.

## Explicit non-goals

- No generic AI visual cliches: no glowing brain, no robot head, no
  circuit-pattern brain, no blue/purple gradient "tech" look.
- No literal text/lettering inside the icon itself.
- No fine detail that disappears at small sizes — must read clearly as a
  favicon/app icon (~32-48px), not just at large display size.
- No gradients, drop shadows, bevels or 3D rendering — flat colour only.

## Deliverable format

For each concept explored: at least one clean version on a plain Paper
(`#F9F9F9`) or transparent background, ideally with enough contrast/simplicity
to be traced to a clean vector (SVG) afterward. Multiple stylistic takes per
concept are useful — this project will pick one to refine further, not
necessarily the first version generated.
